Your mission: turn customer, product, market, and performance evidence into paid-social concepts and copy that designers can execute and media buyers can test.

What You'll Do

  • • Own creative strategy for a focused portfolio of paid-social accounts
  • • Research reviews, calls, competitor ads, prior creative, and product evidence
  • • Find angles rooted in customer psychology, objections, desires, and buying context
  • • Write hooks, headlines, body copy, scripts, CTAs, and useful variations
  • • Turn the strategy into clear, execution-ready briefs
  • • Help designers and creators bring the idea to life without losing the strategy
  • • Review creative for copy, claims, product accuracy, hierarchy, and brief fit
  • • Work with media buyers to learn what worked and plan the next test
  • • Keep research, briefs, feedback, and deadlines organized across accounts

This Will Be For You If

  • • You have owned paid-social or direct-response creative strategy
  • • Your portfolio shows paid ads, not only branding or organic content
  • • You can write persuasive copy, not just suggest visual ideas
  • • You understand customer psychology, offers, awareness, and what makes messages sell
  • • You can read creative performance and recommend what to test next
  • • You enjoy building ideas with designers and media buyers, then improving them together
  • • You plan ahead, meet deadlines, and stay on top of details without being chased
  • • You check sources, product facts, claims, links, and copy before delivery
  • • You communicate confidently in English and can lead client discussions

Do Not Apply If

  • • Your experience is mainly organic content, brand design, or content calendars
  • • You avoid copywriting or rely on someone else to turn your ideas into persuasive language
  • • You treat creative strategy as brainstorming without customer or performance evidence
  • • You use AI output without checking sources, claims, or product accuracy
  • • You miss deadlines, drop feedback, or need constant reminders to stay organized
  • • You prefer working in isolation or competing with teammates over improving the work together
  • • You cannot show what you personally contributed to the work in your portfolio
  • • You cannot work full-time with at least 4 working hours of European-team overlap

Why Join Us

  • Your strategy gets made. Ideas move into real ads instead of dying in a presentation.
  • You see the performance. Learn what sold, what failed, and what to test next.
  • Clear standards. Strong briefs, honest feedback, accurate work, and dependable delivery.
  • Close collaboration. Work directly with Tiago, clients, designers, and media buyers.
  • High standards, low drama. The team focuses on the work, shares context, and solves problems together.
  • 100% remote. Work from anywhere with reliable European-team overlap.
